# Rozpočet a plán vianočného večierka pre školskú družinu v Košiciach

**A well-organized Christmas party for 90 children in a Košice after-school program should cost approximately €800–1,300 in total, or roughly €9–14 per child.** This range covers food, drinks, decorations, craft activities, a tombola, and goodie bags — significantly more generous than the typical Slovak school standard of €3–8 per child. The budget depends heavily on how much parents volunteer (baking, donating tombola prizes) versus how much is purchased outright. Below is a detailed breakdown with real Slovak e-shop prices, a recommended program structure, and three budget tiers.

---

## How 90 kids shape the cost math

The party size estimate of **90 children** (3 grades × 3 classes × ~10 kids per class in družina) is the critical planning variable. Slovak school Christmas parties for this many children work best with a **station-rotation model** — dividing kids into 3–4 groups of ~23 who cycle through craft workshops, games, and snack areas simultaneously. This approach requires more materials but keeps children engaged and manageable.

For context, Slovak parent associations (ZRPŠ) typically collect **€15–30 per child per year** to cover *all* school events. A Christmas party of this scale would consume a meaningful share of that annual fund. In practice, most školská družina Christmas parties rely heavily on **parent in-kind contributions** — homemade pagáčiky, koláčiky, and donated tombola prizes — to keep cash expenditure low. The budget below assumes a mix of purchased and parent-contributed items.

---

## Food, drinks, and treats: €250–380

The food budget is the most flexible category because parent baking contributions can dramatically reduce costs. Here is the breakdown assuming mostly DIY/purchased preparation for 90 children:

**Savory items (€45–70):** Homemade pagáčiky cost roughly €19–27 in ingredients for ~250 pieces (3 per child). DIY mini chlebíčky (open-faced sandwiches) using bread, butter, ham, cheese, and vegetables run €27–42. Pre-made catering platters would cost €600+ for 90 children — far too expensive for a school setting.

**Sweet baked goods (€25–35):** Linecké koláčiky and medovníky are Christmas essentials. Baking from scratch costs €12–15 for linecké cesto ingredients; pre-made medovníčky from cukrovinky.sk cost roughly €1.05 per 95g piece. Budget €25–34 total.

**Fresh fruit (€28–48):** Mandarínky are the quintessential Slovak Christmas fruit at **€0.89–1.99/kg** from Kaufland (buy during pre-Christmas sales). Add jablká, banány, and hrozno for variety. Roughly 10 kg mandarínky + 5 kg each of apples and bananas.

**Table sweets (€30–45):** Salónky (Orion, €2.24/300g pack from cukrovinky.sk), small chocolate Mikuláš figures (€0.30–0.40 each), cukríky mix, and vianočné oblátky (€4.99/20 pieces from farmove.sk).

**Snacks (€20–30):** Slané tyčinky, chipsy in large bags, and oriešky round out the savory side.

**Drinks (€65–105):** Non-alcoholic punč is the star — homemade from ovocný čaj, jablkový džús, pomarančový džús, and spices costs only **€19–28 for 18–20 liters**. Add boxed džúsy (€1.30–2.00/L), minerálka (Rajec or Budiš at €0.50–0.80/bottle), and the kids' favorite — kakao made from Granko (€3–4/450g tin) with 10L of milk.

**Money-saving tip:** Ask each family to contribute one plate of homemade pečivo. With 45–90 families, this alone can eliminate most baking costs.

## Decorations and tableware: €175–280

Disposable tableware for 90 children is the unavoidable core expense in this category. Christmas-themed papierové taniere cost **€2.25 per 6-pack** from party-saurus.sk (15 packs needed = ~€34). Matching poháre run €1.19 per 6-pack (~€18 total). Add vianočné servítky (~€10), obrusy (~€32 for 8 tablecloths at €3.99 each from heliumking.sk), and príbory (~€30 if needed, though finger food eliminates this cost). **Table supplies subtotal: €95–145.**

For room decorations, the budget depends on what the school already owns. Most školy have a small Christmas tree and svetelné reťaze. If purchasing new: a **60–90 cm classroom stromček costs €15–25** from Pepco or Tedi in Košice; plastic ornament sets run €5–10 from OBI. Christmas balóniky (5 packs latex + a few fóliové) total ~€23. Paper girlandy, a "Merry Christmas" banner (€4.79 from heliumking.sk), and papierové dekorácie add ~€30.

A heliumking.sk physical store operates at **Štúrova 14 in Košice**, offering next-day delivery and the largest party supply selection. Budget stores Pepco, Tedi, and Action in Košice offer seasonal items at 50% less than online prices but sell out fast — shop in early November.

**Photo booth props** (vianočné rekvizity na fotenie) cost €10–30 for 2–3 sets of props plus a DIY photo frame. **Decorations subtotal: €80–135.**

## Craft workshops and tombola drive the activity budget: €250–450

Creative workshops (kreatívne dielne) are the most popular activity for this age group and also the largest activity expense. For 90 children making Christmas ornaments at craft stations, materials include:

- **Polystyrénové gule/tvary** for decorating: ~€0.20–0.50 each, 100 pieces = €20–50
- **Drevené vianočné ozdoby** (wooden cutouts to paint): ~€0.20–0.50 each = €20–50
- **Trblietky, korálky, filc** (glitter, beads, felt): €30–60 across various packs
- **Tempery and štetce** (paints and brushes): €45–100 for 8–10 sets
- **Farebný papier, lepidlo** (paper, glue): €23–50
- **Stuhy and špagáty** for hanging ornaments: €5–20

Schools typically already have nožnice, some papier, and basic supplies — reducing the craft budget by €30–50. **Craft materials subtotal: €150–320**, realistically closer to **€180–250** with existing school supplies.

**Tombola** is the party highlight and should be saved for near the end to maintain excitement. Tombola lístky (tickets) cost €2–5 for 100–200 printed tickets or use šatnové bločky. A tombola drum can be DIY'd from a decorated box. The real cost is prizes: **small toys** (€1–3 each from bambule.sk or hracky-4kids.sk), **books** (€2–5 from martinus.sk), and **sweet prizes** (€0.30–0.50 each). For ~30 quality prizes plus 60 consolation sweet prizes, budget **€90–175**. Asking parents to donate unused toys or books can cut this significantly.

**Games and music** are inexpensive: stoličková hra uses school chairs and a Bluetooth speaker; balóniky for dance games cost €5–8. Game prizes (stickers, small candies) run €15–40. A budget Bluetooth speaker costs €30–80 to purchase, or borrow from a teacher. **Games + music subtotal: €50–130.**

## Goodie bags at €2.50–3.50 per child are the sweet spot

DIY vianočné balíčky offer the best value for a school budget. The recommended contents per child:

- 1× čokoládový Mikuláš (40–60g): €0.50–0.80
- 3–4× salónky or čokoládové bonbóny: €0.20–0.30
- 1× candy cane lízatko (€0.25 from cukrovinky.sk)
- 1× perníček: €0.30–0.50
- 1× mandarínka: €0.15–0.20
- Handful of cukríky: €0.15–0.25
- 1× small creative gift (nálepky, omaľovánka, vianočné pero): €0.50–1.00
- Papierové vrecúško with Christmas motif: €0.15–0.30

**Total per child: €2.20–3.60.** For 90 children: **€200–325.** The middle estimate of €250 (€2.80/child) produces a satisfying balíček. Buy sladkosti in bulk from cukrovinky.sk (free delivery over €60) and vrecúška in 100-piece packs for the lowest per-unit cost.

Pre-made Mikulášske balíčky from cukrovinky.sk cost **€4.49–4.99 each** (€404–449 for 90 children) — a convenient but pricier alternative. The municipal standard in many Slovak towns is roughly **€5 per child** for a Mikuláš package.

## The recommended 3-hour party program

Based on Slovak educator resources from eduworld.sk and zborovna.sk, the ideal školská družina vianočný večierok runs **2.5–3 hours** (typically 14:00–17:00) and follows this structure:

| Time | Activity | Duration |
|------|----------|----------|
| 14:00–14:15 | **Privítanie a vianočný program** — Welcome, group singing of "Tichá noc" or "Nesiem vám noviny," short recitations of vianočné básne and vinše prepared in advance | 15 min |
| 14:15–15:00 | **Kreatívne dielne** — 3–4 craft stations with groups rotating every 12–15 min. Station ideas: polystyrénové ozdoby, papierové vločky/reťaze, maľovanie drevených ozdôb, výroba vianočných pohľadníc | 45 min |
| 15:00–15:20 | **Občerstvenie** — Christmas treats buffet with punč, kakao, koláčiky, ovocie | 20 min |
| 15:20–15:50 | **Vianočné hry** — Stoličková hra, freeze dance, relay races ("snehuliakovo obliekanie"), blindfolded taste-testing of Christmas foods, vianočný kvíz | 30 min |
| 15:50–16:15 | **Tombola** — The highlight: drawing numbers, awarding prizes with ceremony and cheering | 25 min |
| 16:15–16:30 | **Photo booth a voľná zábava** — Funny Christmas photos with rekvizity, free play | 15 min |
| 16:30–16:50 | **Koledy a záver** — Group singing of traditional carols, final wishes | 20 min |
| 16:50–17:00 | **Rozlúčka** — Handing out vianočné balíčky, goodbye | 10 min |

**Essential logistics for 90 children:** The station-rotation model requires **4–6 supervising adults** (vychovávateľky plus parent volunteers). Each craft station needs 1–2 adults. Set up the photo booth to run concurrently during craft time or snack time. Prepare tombola tickets and prizes in advance, and let children decorate the classroom stromček together as an opening activity.

Slovak traditions worth incorporating include **zdobenie stromčeka** as a group activity, the **vianočný tanier** tradition (a plate of mandarínky, oriešky, sušené ovocie, and medovníčky), and potentially a **"Krabica od topánok"** charity element where children prepare shoebox gifts for seniors — a widely practiced Slovak school tradition.

## Three budget tiers for 90 children

| Category | Úsporný (Budget) | Stredný (Standard) | Komfortný (Comfortable) |
|----------|------------------|--------------------|-----------------------|
| Food & drinks | €190 | €315 | €385 |
| Goodie bags | €200 | €255 | €325 |
| Table supplies | €95 | €125 | €145 |
| Decorations | €60 | €100 | €135 |
| Craft materials | €150 | €220 | €320 |
| Tombola (tickets + prizes) | €70 | €130 | €175 |
| Games + music | €35 | €75 | €130 |
| Photo booth + other | €20 | €40 | €60 |
| **Total** | **€820** | **€1,260** | **€1,675** |
| **Per child** | **€9.11** | **€14.00** | **€18.61** |

The **standard tier at ~€1,260 (€14/child)** represents a genuinely festive party with quality craft activities, a real tombola, good food spread, and solid goodie bags. The **budget tier at ~€820 (€9/child)** is achievable if parents contribute baked goods and tombola prizes — reducing the cash outlay to roughly **€550–650** in purchased items.

## Conclusion

The biggest cost drivers are, in descending order: goodie bags, craft materials, tombola prizes, and disposable tableware. These four categories alone account for roughly 60% of the total budget. The single most effective cost-reduction strategy is **parent mobilization** — families baking pagáčiky and koláčiky, donating unused toys for tombola, and volunteering as station supervisors. With strong parent involvement, the cash budget can drop to **€6–8 per child** while still delivering a memorable vianočný večierok. Order Christmas-themed supplies from heliumking.sk (with their Košice store for pickup), buy sladkosti in bulk from cukrovinky.sk, and hit Kaufland during pre-Christmas mandarínky sales. Start collecting tombola prizes and organizing parent baking sign-ups by mid-November.
