MPLS

-------------------------

mpls = multi protocol label switching

chceme sa vyhnut aby sme vsade mali bgp routovaciu tbaulku etc
a mpls nam to pomoze


mpls nebude ip forwarding, nebude preposielat na  zakl. ip
ale na zaklade label
taze kam posleme packet na zakl. label a nie ip adresy

multi protocol = means that it is not important what kind of packet we send
so no important what kind of routovany protokol it uses

routovane protokoly - ipv4 ipv6

so:
sending based on labels
no matter if they have ipv4 or ipv6
